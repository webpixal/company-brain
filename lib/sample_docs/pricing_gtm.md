# Needletail Pricing & GTM Playbook

## Pricing Tiers
Needletail offers three tiers for dental offices:
- **Starter** ($299/mo): Up to 2 chairs, AI call answering, basic scheduling sync.
- **Growth** ($699/mo): Up to 6 chairs, AI call answering + patient recall campaigns, insurance verification assist.
- **Scale** ($1,499/mo): Unlimited chairs, full human-in-the-loop escalation queue, dedicated onboarding specialist, custom integrations.

## Sales Motion
Our GTM motion is outbound-led for Growth/Scale (targeting DSOs with 5+ locations) and inbound-led for Starter (single-practice owners finding us via SEO/content).

Average sales cycle: 18 days for Starter, 45 days for Scale (requires IT/ops sign-off at DSO level).

## Common Objections
1. "We already use a call answering service" — Reframe: Needletail integrates directly with the PMS (practice management software) so bookings sync automatically; generic answering services can't do that.
2. "What happens when the AI can't handle a call?" — Explain the human-in-the-loop escalation: flagged calls route to a live agent within 90 seconds, visible in the dashboard.
3. "Is patient data secure?" — HIPAA-compliant, data encrypted at rest and in transit, BAAs available on request.

## Onboarding SLA
New Growth/Scale customers get a dedicated specialist for the first 14 days. PMS integration typically completes within 3 business days of contract signature.
